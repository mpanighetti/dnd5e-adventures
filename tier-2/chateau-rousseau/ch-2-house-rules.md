# Chapter 2: Suggested House Rules

Player death is always a possibility in Dungeons & Dragons, but Chateau Rousseau's lack of safe spaces and a threat actively hunting the party means it particularly indulges in taking the life of its inhabitants. To that end, a few house rules regarding character creation and running the game are recommended:

- Using the **Starting Equipment at Higher Levels** guidance from the _SRD_, allow each party member to start with 500 GP + 1d10 × 25 GP and a Common and Uncommon magic item of their choice, in addition to their normal starting equipment.
  - Any gold in a player's starting equipment can be spent before the adventure on additional equipment if desired.
  - In order to simplify spellcasting, spells that consume Material components of value are satisfied by consuming an equivalent amount of gold.
- Attuning to items works as normal, but Attunement takes 1 minute instead of requiring a Short Rest.

If a player character dies, the character immediately returns as a spirit. They regain all spell slots, and their other features are restored as if they completed a Long Rest, with the following changes:

- Their Hit Point Maximum is halved.
- Their creature type changes to Undead.
- When Goren attempts to find or attack a spirit, they are considered to be in a Heavily Obscured area, as he has difficulty perceiving spirits.
- If a spirit is reduced to 0 Hit Points again, they are destroyed and cannot be restored to life short of divine intervention, the _Wish_ spell, or similarly powerful features.

If the party succeeds in escaping Chateau Rousseau or defeating Goren, a player's spirit comes back to life and leaves with the rest of the party, unless the spirit were destroyed.
